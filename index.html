Named After a Mile of Road

Eglinton Avenue East was branded the Golden Mile in the 1950s, when it was Canada’s first planned industrial strip and the fastest-growing stretch of road in the country.

We are still on it, at number 1743. Every vehicle on this page is parked here, and you are welcome to come and sit in all eight of them.
